# Marketing Campaign & Customer Behavior Analysis



## 📌 Project Overview

This project provides an end-to-end analysis of customer personality and marketing campaign performance. By leveraging **Python** for data engineering and **Power BI** for business intelligence, I transformed raw, fragmented data into actionable insights regarding customer spending habits, channel preferences, and demographic trends.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Workflow

The project follows a modular pipeline to ensure data integrity and high-performance reporting:

1. **Data Engineering (Python/Pandas):** * Cleaned and normalized customer demographic data.
* Engineered features such as **Total Amount Spent** and **Total Children**.
* Standardized the **Customer Joining Date** and handled **Annual Income** outliers.
* Exported a lean, optimized CSV for Power BI consumption.


2. **Data Visualization (Power BI):**
* Developed an interactive dashboard with advanced **DAX** measures.
* Visualized performance across **Web, Catalog, and Store Purchases**.
* Implemented dynamic filtering by **Education Level** and **Marital Status**.



## 📈 Key Metrics Tracked

* **Customer Lifetime Value:** Aggregated total spend per customer.
* **Channel Attribution:** Comparison of conversion volume across physical and digital storefronts.
* **Web Engagement:** Relationship between **Monthly Website Visits** and actual purchases.
* **Retention:** Analysis of **Days Since Last Purchase** to identify churn risk.

## 🚀 Strategic Insights

* **Segment Targeting:** Identified high-income brackets (70k+) with a direct correlation to premium product spend.
* **Web Conversion:** Revealed segments with high web traffic but low conversion rates, highlighting a need for UI/UX optimization.
* **Educational Correlation:** Proved that customers with "Graduation" or "PhD" levels represent the highest total revenue contributors.
